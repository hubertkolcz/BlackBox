# ESSAY-005 — delegation-ready problem specification (2026-07-13)

The project's stated priority question: **can the Abramsky–Brandenburger (AB) sheaf machinery *derive* the Global-Exclusivity (GE) composed bound for graphs built as products of KCBS 5-cycles — i.e., calculate local–global relations of GE product graphs — rather than merely describe the obstruction after the bound is known?** (`ESSAY-005`; obstacle located in `SH-006`.) This note fixes the mathematical target precisely and specifies four probes with acceptance criteria, so that any delegated computation attacks the right object. Two wrong-target traps are called out first, because each would silently waste an entire compute delegation.

## Trap 1 — the target value is the GE *sequence*, not ϑ(G)

"GE composed bound" for k identical copies of G is the exclusivity-LP value on the k-fold conormal product with the product ansatz — call it S_k(G). For the pentagon: S₁(C₅) = α\* = 5/2 and S₂(C₅) = √5 (Cabello's two-copy derivation; `GE-001`). But **for C₇, C₉ the sequence does not move**: S₁ = S₂ = S₃ = α\* (our `GE-002`, now theorems of Choudhary–Barbosa arXiv:2411.09773, Thms 10/12 — see `open-search-frontier/d1-external-reconciliation-2026-07-13.md`). Any candidate cohomological invariant must therefore be scored against **S_k(G)**, not against ϑ(G): an invariant that "predicts" ϑ(C₇) ≈ 3.318 at k = 2,3 is *wrong* (the true GE value there is 3.5), even though for the pentagon at k = 2 the two targets coincide at √5. Score candidates on the pair {C₅: 5/2 → √5} AND the negative control {C₇: 3.5 → 3.5 → 3.5}.

## Trap 2 — the level mismatch (possibilistic vs probabilistic)

The paclet's Čech layer (`CechObstruction`, `CechCohomology`, `AvNArgument`) computes invariants of the **support presheaf** — possibilistic data. The classical-optimal and quantum-optimal pentagon models have *identical supports* (all 15 sections; `SH-001` stratum data), so **every support-level invariant is constant across the entire classical↔quantum↔α\* probability range and cannot output √5**. This is not a conjecture; it follows from the supports being equal. Any derivation of S_k must therefore either (a) build a *weighted/probabilistic* presheaf (values in [0,1]-semimodules, not Z-modules) — machinery that does **not** yet exist in the paclet — or (b) extract S_k from the *combinatorial structure of the cover itself* (the pentad clique data), with cohomology contributing the local-global bookkeeping. Probes P1/P4 below make this boundary experimentally explicit before any tool-building is committed.

## Objects (all already computable here)

Cover for one copy: contexts = the 5 edges of C₅ (maximal cliques of the exclusivity graph). Cover for two copies: the conormal-product cover on 25 events — `ab_sheaf.wl` / `SupportCohomology.wl` already build it (two-copy product cover, |S_e| = 11² check); the pentad partition (5 disjoint 5-cliques {(i, 2i+j mod 5)}) is the combinatorial engine of the √5 proof, and the full maximal-clique census of C₅∨C₅ (535 maximal cliques; `GE-001`) is in `CertifyingQuantumness.wl`. Paclet: `CoverScenario` accepts arbitrary covers; `GlobalSectionQ`, `ContextualFraction` give the LP layer.

## Probes

**P1 — negative control (hours, no new code).** Compute the Čech obstruction census on the two-copy product cover for models along the path Σp ∈ {2, √5, 5/2} (classical max → quantum → α\* point, e.g. the V-parametrized family plus the Wright-type point). *Expected:* invariants constant wherever supports coincide — confirming Trap 2 concretely. *Acceptance:* if ANY support-level invariant moves at √5, Trap 2 is refuted and that invariant becomes the research object (major surprise); if none moves, the possibilistic route to S_k is CLOSED with a citable computation, and all further effort goes to (a)/(b). Either way no compute is wasted.

**P2 — cover-type obstacle, made exhaustive (days, pure combinatorics).** `SH-006`'s obstacle: AB's KS-type results (their Prop. 9.3/9.4) apply to covers that are maximal-clique covers of the complement of an *equivalence relation* ("partition-type"; Bell scenarios), and C₅'s edge cover is not partition-type. Formalize P(cover) := "arises from an equivalence-relation complement" as an executable predicate; exhaustively search covers on ≤ 10 measurements for one that (i) satisfies P and (ii) has C₅ as its exclusivity quotient (events = compatible joint outcomes, exclusivity graph isomorphic to C₅ with matching probabilistic structure). *Expected:* none exists. *Acceptance:* an exhaustion certificate = the impossibility half of ESSAY-005's "either answer is a result" at bounded size (state the bound honestly); a counterexample = the construction half — either outcome publishable. This is a finite, delegable search with exact acceptance conditions.

**P3 — the product-ansatz LP as a gluing statement (a week, formalization + small LP).** The two-copy derivation is: local normalization on each pentad 5-clique + product structure p⊗p ⇒ (Σp)² ≤ 5. Candidate sheaf reformulation to test: define the presheaf of *subnormalized weightings* on the product cover (sections over clique C = w: C → [0,1], Σ_C w ≤ 1; restrictions = marginal agreement on shared events); the GE bound is then: max total weight of a *global* section family compatible with the product ansatz. Claim to verify mechanically: for C₅ this LP equals √5 at k=2 and for C₇ it equals 3.5 at k=2,3 (matching S_k; the pentad partition appears as an exact fractional clique cover certificate = a specific Čech 0-cochain). *Acceptance:* if the LP-with-gluing formulation reproduces the S_k sequence on both C₅ and the C₇ control, ESSAY-005's "derivation" reduces to: which cohomological invariant of the weighted presheaf computes this LP's value? — a sharply-posed question for the weighted-cohomology tool-building in (a). If it fails on C₇, the ansatz encoding is wrong and must be fixed before ANY tool-building.

**P4 — torsion vs. GE-value scan (cheap, bounded).** Across the census of models on the product cover, tabulate obstruction-order spectra (`ObstructionOrder`, `CechRelativeCohomology`) against Σp. *Expected from Trap 2:* flat wherever supports agree; the only movement is at support changes (e.g., entering the Wright stratum). *Acceptance:* confirms and quantifies exactly where possibilistic invariants gain/lose sight of the GE value; the resulting table is the essay's honest "what cohomology can and cannot see" exhibit, complementing `SH-005`'s torsion dichotomy.

## What would count as answering ESSAY-005

Either: (i) a weighted-presheaf invariant (built per P3's reduction) whose value on C₅^(∨k) provably equals S_k and which is functorial enough to transfer to other graphs (validated on the C₇ negative control and, as a stretch target, on Quad-C₅ = arXiv:2605.12828, whose graph is now isomorphism-confirmed in `pentagon-gluing/quad_c5_crosscheck.py`); or (ii) a bounded-size impossibility statement per P2 plus the P1/P4 closure of the possibilistic route — which together would justify the essay's claim that *describing* (obstruction) and *deriving* (bound) are separated in principle for GE covers, with the separation located exactly at the possibilistic/probabilistic divide.

## Cost discipline

P1 and P4 reuse existing paclet functions verbatim (no new mathematics — safe to delegate immediately). P2 needs only the predicate formalization reviewed once by hand before the search runs (review the equivalence-relation encoding against AB's §9 text — one hour, prevents the whole search targeting a wrong predicate). P3's LP is small (25–125 events) but its *encoding* is the step to get right: have the product-ansatz constraint set derived on paper and checked against the known values (5/2, √5, 3.5) before scaling anywhere.

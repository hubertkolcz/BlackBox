# MeshGlue Essay — Terminology Analysis & Treatment Assessment

**Date:** 2026-07-16 · **Applies to:** MeshGlue.nb (base commit 86ce437, deployed to CloudObject["MeshGlue-PentagonGluing"])

## Method

Five parallel scanning passes swept every textual cell of the essay under distinct lenses (quantum foundations; graph theory; optimization & certificates; topology & geometry; quantum-computing primitives), producing ~96 raw candidate reports. A merging editorial pass deduplicated them and judged each candidate against two criteria: (1) the term is genuinely advanced — a scientifically literate non-specialist could stumble on it; (2) an exact definition adds rigor beyond what the essay prose already gives. The new closing section (From Gluing Words to Black-Box Certification, added in 86ce437) received its own dedicated sweep. Every accepted term then got a definition written in the author-voice (no verbatim copying), anchored to one respected source — Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy for foundational concepts, standard monographs or the original defining paper for mathematics/physics, never Wikipedia — with the source URL fetched and checked live, followed by an independent adversarial verification pass (exactness, essay-convention consistency, no verbatim runs, citation validity).

**Outcome:** 60 terms received hidden definition fields; 44 candidates were assessed and excluded. Sources: 55 unique references (11 SEP entries, 23 monographs, 26 papers), all appended to the notebook References section.

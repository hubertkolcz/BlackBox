## 1. The objectives, and whether the repository meets them

Research title: **Evaluating black-box physics through optical emulation.** The description commits the project to five things: (O1) correlation-based classification of measurement statistics; (O2) Lie-algebraic criteria for resource scaling; (O3) conditions under which a quantum device is mathematically indistinguishable from a classical optical emulation at the input–output level; (O4) illustration on Hawking-radiation dynamics; (O5) feasibility of extending the correlation taxonomy to signaling/quantum-communication scenarios — plus a stated priority question (can Abramsky–Brandenburger sheaf cohomology *derive* local–global relations of GE graphs built as products of KCBS 5-cycles?) and a stated deliverable form (a WSRI computational essay with every quantitative claim kernel-verified).

Verdict per objective:

**O1 — delivered, mature.** The D2 track is the strongest part of the corpus: exact pentagon invariants (α=2, ϑ=√5, α\*=5/2), the GE mechanism at two copies reproduced with SDP certificates (`GE-001`), the contextual fraction with primal+dual LP certificates and its exact relation to signed negativity (`CF-001`–`CF-004`, including the unconditional odd-n-cycle proof in `CF-negativity-proof.md`), all wired into machine-checked `…Verification` associations with headless runners. The correlation-classification layer the title promises exists, is exact where exactness is claimed, and is reproducible by one command per module.

**O2 — partially delivered, and the honest core result is negative.** The Lie-algebraic side exists as the `LP-001` so(3)/DLA interface in the paclet, the `LP-002` decorrelation study (DLA growth and CF density do not co-move under composition — the evidence that two lenses are non-redundant), and `LP-003` (the DLA computation hits the generic 4ⁿ−1 wall past ~2 pentagons). What is *not* yet done: scoping these claims against the DLA-simulability literature (see §4). The repo's central O2 claim — "an intensity-redistribution rig is leaf-confined, DLA < 3, hence detectable" — is currently asserted at the interface level but not yet formalized as a theorem with stated observable-class assumptions. That formalization is now the single most important theory task on O2, because the literature (Somma et al.; g-sim; Cerezo et al. 2312.09121; Aaronson–Arkhipov as the contrast case) makes precise *which* caveats such a claim must carry: poly-DLA implies efficient classical estimation only for algebra-supported observables, and small DLA does not preclude sampling hardness.

**O3 — operationalized; the impossibility condition is located but not yet stated as a theorem.** `00-BBT-blackbox-protocol/` is a genuine pre-registered certification protocol (962 lines, fixed thresholds, confidence budget, seven gates, master seed) that correctly separates quantum, NCHV, and intensity-emulator boxes from finite click statistics — with one pre-declared, structural blind spot (`BBT-002`): an intensity emulator tuned exactly to the quantum table is table-level indistinguishable *by construction*. That blind spot **is** the answer to the project's central question ("under what conditions is distinction mathematically impossible?") in embryo: at the level of single-context tables, the impossibility condition is exact tuning to the quantum point; the escape is either event-semantics (click statistics, per Markiewicz et al.) or the second lens (DLA audit). The literature pass strengthens this: Kovtoniuk–Bohmann–Semenov (arXiv:2601.13869) prove any click record from a single unmodified on-off detector is coherent-state-forgeable — the detector-level formal statement of exactly this blind spot — and Frustaglia et al. (PRL 116, 250404) exhibit classical fields *reaching* √5. What remains for O3 is to write the indistinguishability statement as a standalone, citable proposition (assumptions: table access only, semantics unverified, no attenuation stage) with the two escape routes as named corollaries. The raw material is all present.

**O4 — delivered, as a structural negative result, and the literature search confirms it stands on unclaimed ground.** `HK-003` (the Cauchy–Schwarz witness used by real analogue-Hawking experiments is single-context by construction, so CF ≡ 0 at every discretization) plus `HK-004` (Gaussian states foreclose the Wigner route, Hudson) is a real scope theorem. No published work applies contextuality measures to analogue Hawking certification; the nearest escalation beyond second moments is Bell-type (Ciliberto–Emig–Pavloff–Isoard, PRA 109, 063325 (2024)) — nonlocality, not contextuality. Written up honestly ("the graph-invariant lens cannot even be posed for this witness class"), this is a publishable boundary result and the correct illustration of the framework's reach.

**O5 — feasibility delivered.** Three orthogonal gates verified on 7 exemplars (`SIG-001`), an exact communication-cost identity at the quantum KCBS point (`SIG-002`), and a refuted candidate identity (`SIG-003`) — the last being a model example of the project's own numerics catching an over-generalization. Feasibility is what the description promised; a full signaling taxonomy remains future work and is correctly not claimed.

**Priority question (AB-sheaf derivation over GE products) — open, with the obstacle precisely identified.** `SH-006` pins why this is hard (AB Prop. 9.3/9.4: Bell-type measurement covers and CSW exclusivity covers are different combinatorial objects), `SH-005` supplies the raw material (the ten maximal 5-cliques of C₅∨C₅, exact torsion orders), and the roadmap states the two acceptable outcomes (construct a GE-side cover the argument applies to, or prove none exists). This is the right shape for an open problem: not unattempted, but blocked at a named proposition. It should be the essay's stated open frontier.

**Deliverable form — infrastructure done, essay integration pending.** Machine-checked verification associations, headless runners, `RunAll.ps1`, the paclet with tests and documentation pages, and the project-side `ComputationalEssay.nb`/`EssayTemplate.nb` all exist. What does not yet exist is the wiring that makes the essay *pull* its numbers from the kernel-verified modules rather than restate them — the specific WSRI requirement. That is an assembly task, not a research task, and it is the natural endpoint of this review's recommendations.

**Overall:** the repository substantively meets O1, O4, O5, operationalizes O3 to within one formalization step, and meets O2 halfway with an honest negative finding where a naive unified-criterion hope would have been. The gap between "research done" and "research reportable" is now mostly organizational, which is what §5 addresses.

## 2. Module-by-module assessment

**`00-BBT-blackbox-protocol/` (primary).** Right to be module 00; the README correctly states dependency direction (inward only) and the blind spot. [Corrected 2026-07-13, same day:] the geometric lens **is** executable — gate G7 computes the DLA audit (span-2 → dim-3 cascade anchor via the paclet's `CascadeGenerators`/`DLADimension`; single-axis compilation flagged leaf-confined, DLA < 3). The honest residual gap is input semantics, not implementation: G7 consumes a *claimed mode compilation* (white-box declaration), so against a fully black-box adversary the two-lens closure of `BBT-002` rests on the device operator's compilation claim being auditable. The next increment is therefore not "add a gate" but: state G7's trust assumption explicitly, and investigate what (if anything) bounds the DLA from black-box data alone — see `00-BBT-blackbox-protocol/PROPOSITION-O3.md`.

**`01-D2-core-computation/`.** The bedrock; strongest verification culture in the repo (13-section circuit essay with Heisenberg-picture identity checks at 1e−16, exact symbolic geometry, dual LP certificates). Two uncommitted files (`kcbs_circuit_ncycle.wl`, `kcbs_sequential_game.wl`) belong to `FOUND-003`/`FOUND-004` and must be committed for the ledger references to resolve.

**`02-D1-theory-frontier/`.** Correctly scoped "numerics only." Externally, this track is the most exposed to being scooped or contradicted: Choudhary–Barbosa (arXiv:2411.09773) prove Ramsey-theoretically that n≥6-cycle PR boxes show *no* E-principle activation at 2–3 copies — overlapping the track's own C7/C9 zero-improvement findings (`GE-002`). This is simultaneously validation (independent agreement) and a citation duty (their proofs subsume some of the track's numerics). Engage it explicitly in the D1 notes; reposition D1's residual value as the *vertex-transitive bracket census* (`GE-003`) and the catalysis theorems (`ERG-001`/`002`), which they do not cover.

**`03-MESH-pentagon-composition/`.** The cis/trans discovery, τ\* closed form, (cct)^∞ optimum, and the trans-chain proofs are the repo's most distinctive mathematics; the prior-art audit (0 established / 5 adjacent / 2 novel) is credible and already folded into the claims. Residual weakness: `MESH-006` (Quad-C₅ reconstruction) is still flagged isomorphism-unconfirmed against arXiv:2605.12828 — resolve before the essay uses it as a benchmark; it is a one-afternoon check.

**`04-cluster-state-mbqc/` (entirely uncommitted).** Scientifically valuable (sparse GF(2) stabilizer verification to ~10⁶+ qubits, block-local AvN witness, DLA wall confirmed independently) and correctly honest about the three different validity radii. **`05-CERT-epsilon-certificates/`.** Good separation of certificates, generators, and `debug/`. Two provenance issues, both already self-flagged: k=7/k=8 certificates have no surviving generator (`ISSUE-001` — regenerate and diff, this is the last unreconstructed-provenance gap), and `GenerateEpsilonCertificate10_cloud.wl` exported to the k=9 filename (`ISSUE-020` — **fixed in-place with this review**, 2026-07-13; verify in the diff at commit time). `extract_pdf.wl` (hardcoded expired path) and `glv_paper_text.txt` are historical artifacts that belong in an attic, not next to live certificates.

**`06-D3-sheaf-cohomology/`.** Small folder, big content (Laplacian rejection by pre-registered gate; Čech obstruction with exact torsion orders; torsion dichotomy parity-vs-box). The pre-registered *rejection* of the cellular-sheaf Laplacian (`SH-004`) is exemplary practice and should be a highlighted section of the essay, not a footnote — negative gate results are the project's most credible methodological signature.

**`07-SIG-signaling/` and `08-HK-hawking/`.** Both correctly scoped as independent extensions. `08-HK` carries a byte-identical duplicate (`NOTES-hawking-2-precommit-draft.md` = `NOTES-hawking-2.md`) — delete the draft. ## 3. Verification and reproducibility audit

What is already right — and unusually so for a solo research repo: every module ends in a machine-checkable verification association; runners exist for headless execution; exact arithmetic (ℚ(√5), `RootReduce`, SNF torsion orders, rational SDP certificates) is used where claims are exact; pre-registration discipline (protocol thresholds, gate tests for the Laplacian) is applied; adversarial novelty audits are recorded *inside* the claims; withdrawn claims (the cis/trans bracket) stay visible with their correction. This is the reproducibility spine most published computational papers lack.

Gaps, in priority order:

1. **Uncommitted work.** 16 files including all of module 04. Additionally `.git/index.lock` exists and is not removable from this sandboxed session (a stale lock — likely from an interrupted git process; also note a harmless empty file `.git/_writetest` left by this session's write-permission probe; delete both). Until the lock is removed, *no* git operation will succeed. See `MAINTENANCE.md` for the exact recovery + commit sequence.
2. **No dependency manifest.** Python needs `numpy, scipy, python-igraph, cvxpy, clarabel, sympy` (from actual imports); nothing declared them. `requirements.txt` added. Wolfram needs WL 13+ plus `Wolfram/QuantumFramework` (auto-installed by essays) — now stated once in the README.
3. **No citation metadata.** `CITATION.cff` added (repo-level; update `preferred-citation` when the essay/paper acquires an identifier).
4. **Environment capture.** No record of WL version / OS / solver versions per verification sweep. Cheap fix: have `RunAll` print `$Version`, paclet versions, and date into a `verification-log-<date>.txt` artifact, and commit those logs. This converts "OK -> True on my machine" into an auditable record.
5. **The ledger lives outside the repo.** For external readers the repo is the unit of reproduction; a dated snapshot of `LEDGER.md`/`KNOWN_ISSUES.md` is now mirrored under `docs/ledger-snapshot/` with provenance headers. Long-term: move the canonical ledger *into* the repo (it indexes this repo's files) and let the Documents-side project reference it, not vice versa.
6. **CI.** Wolfram licensing makes hosted CI nontrivial; the realistic tier is: GitHub Actions running (a) the pure-Python verifications (`trans_chain_proofs.py`, `cf_nu.py` fast paths, protocol smoke test at reduced N) and (b) link/manifest checks, with the WL battery remaining a locally-run, log-committed step. Recommended, not yet implemented (needs the repo on GitHub first — which is itself an open thread: decide `hubertkolcz` vs WaverQ org, then `gh repo create`).

## 4. Positioning against the literature (fresh pass, 2026-07-13)

Full annotated bibliography: `RELATED-WORK.md`. The findings that change how the work should be reported:

**The two-lens combination appears to be genuinely unclaimed.** Correlation-side certification (self-testing: Bharti et al. PRL 122, 250403; experimental single-system self-test: Hu et al. npj QI 9, 103; photonic-processor certification: Giordani et al. Sci. Adv. 9, eadj4249) and Lie-algebraic simulability (Somma et al.; g-sim arXiv:2308.01432; Ragone et al.; Cerezo et al. arXiv:2312.09121) are mature but disjoint communities. The only DLA-flavoured device-verification work found is Rodari et al. (arXiv:2505.03001, Lie-algebraic invariants as boson-sampler benchmarks) — no contextuality component. Conversely, the existing "contextuality prices classical simulation" line (Kleinmann et al. NJP 13, 113011; Karanjai–Wallman–Bartlett arXiv:1802.07744; Trandafir–Kelleher–Cabello arXiv:2506.06869) prices emulation in *memory bits derived from the correlations themselves*, never in the generator algebra of the device. The essay must cite that memory-cost line as the incumbent and state the differentiator explicitly: the DLA lens prices the *dynamics*, is computable from a compilation audit rather than from the correlations, and is exactly what survives when the correlations are perfectly forged (`BBT-002`).

**The adversary is published and sharp — use it.** Frustaglia et al. (PRL 116, 250404 (2016)): classical microwave circuits reproduce KCBS/CHSH statistics up to the quantum bounds, √5 included. Together with Zhang et al. (already cited) and the new Kovtoniuk–Bohmann–Semenov single-detector forgeability theorem (arXiv:2601.13869) and Korolkova–Sánchez-Soto–Leuchs's operational boundary (arXiv:2405.15692), the intensity-emulator adversary of `BBT-001/002` is not a strawman but the literature's own state of the art. Catani et al. (Quantum 7, 1119) and Hance–Krnic–Larsson (arXiv:2601.13109) supply the conceptual frame (interference phenomenology is noncontextually modelable; KS-contextuality is the nonclassical core) that says the project bet on the right invariant.

**O2 claims need literature-shaped guardrails.** Required scoping when writing up LP-001/002/003: (i) poly-DLA ⇒ efficient classical simulation holds for expectation values of algebra-supported observables (g-sim; Bärligea et al. arXiv:2604.16701 for representation-sensitivity), not for sampling — passive linear optics has dim u(m)=m² yet boson sampling is hard (Aaronson–Arkhipov); (ii) the strongest simulability statements assume an initial quantum data-acquisition phase (Cerezo et al.); (iii) DLA dimension is not a complete trainability/simulability parameter (Diaz et al.). None of these break the project's use — "DLA < 3 flags a leaf-confined rig" is an *emulator-side* upper-bound argument, which is the safe direction — but the essay must say so before a referee does.

**D1 must engage Choudhary–Barbosa (arXiv:2411.09773) and the almost-quantum boundary** (Navascués et al., Nat. Commun. 6, 6288): finite-copy exclusivity provably fails to activate for n≥6 cycles, and almost-quantum satisfies one-copy exclusivity — so D1's open question ("does GE-with-copies single out ϑ for every graph?") is not just open but now has named obstructions on both sides. Cabello 2019 (arXiv:1801.06347) and Nogueira–Vieira–Terra Cunha (PRA 111, 052418 (2025)) give the strongest positive conditional results and belong next to arXiv:1210.2988 in the foundations list.

**Hawking chapter: the citation set is settled.** Steinhauer 2016 + Leonhardt critique + Steinhauer reply; Busch–Parentani and de Nova–Sols–Zapata for the CS witness's definition (the latter explicitly concede the field's restriction to quadratic witnesses — direct textual support for HK-003); Isoard–Pavloff; Kolobov et al. 2021; Agullo–Brady–Kranas for the Gaussian-fragility frontier; Ciliberto et al. 2024 as the only beyond-second-moment escalation (Bell, not contextuality); Hudson + Bartlett et al. (PRL 88, 097904) as the classical-simulability anchors. The chapter's thesis — contextuality-based certification *cannot be posed* for the witness class actually in use — is confirmed unclaimed.

## 5. Organization: what the ideal repository looks like

The goal-based module layout (00–08 + paclet + runners) is correct and should not be churned. The ideal structure is the current one plus a **reporting layer** and **hygiene**, minus **dead weight**:

```
black-box/
├── README.md               ← title, description, quickstart, module map, verification table (REWRITTEN)
├── RESEARCH.md             ← verbatim objectives; objective→module→claim→runner matrix; status (NEW)
├── CITATION.cff            ← citation metadata (NEW)
├── requirements.txt        ← Python deps (NEW)
├── LICENSE, .gitattributes, .gitignore
├── docs/
│   ├── QUANTUM_CONTEXTUALITY.md      ← narrative log (append-only; not a status doc)
│   ├── RELATED-WORK.md               ← curated bibliography w/ MUST-CITE flags (NEW)
│   ├── REVIEW-2026-07-13.md          ← this document (NEW)
│   ├── MAINTENANCE.md                ← git recovery, commit plan, release checklist (NEW)
│   └── ledger-snapshot/              ← dated LEDGER.md + KNOWN_ISSUES.md mirrors (NEW)
├── 00-BBT-blackbox-protocol/         ← + (future) gate 8: executable DLA audit stub
├── 01-D2-core-computation/
├── 02-D1-theory-frontier/            ← + engage arXiv:2411.09773 in the notes
├── 03-MESH-pentagon-composition/
├── 04-cluster-state-mbqc/            ← COMMIT IT (name: consider 04-MBQC-cluster-state for track-key consistency — optional, do it in a dedicated rename commit or not at all)
├── 05-CERT-epsilon-certificates/     ← fix ISSUE-020 strings before committing the k=10 generator
├── 06-D3-sheaf-cohomology/
├── 07-SIG-signaling/
├── 08-HK-hawking/                    ← delete the byte-identical precommit-draft duplicate
├── zz-attic/                         ← (RECOMMENDED) extract_pdf.wl, glv_paper_text.txt, other historical artifacts
├── BlackBox/                         ← paclet; PacletInfo is the single version authority
└── runners/                          ← RunAll.ps1 (parametrize $ws), RunAll.sh (NEW), Run<Name>.wl
```

Principles behind it, so future additions stay coherent:

1. **One status spine.** Objectives live in `RESEARCH.md`; claims live in the ledger; narrative lives in `docs/QUANTUM_CONTEXTUALITY.md`; each references the others and none restates numbers it doesn't own. Stale-number drift (paclet v1.1.0/v1.6.0/27/29) is the failure mode this prevents.
2. **The repo is the unit of reproduction.** Anything an external reader needs to check a claim — ledger snapshot, dependency manifests, run commands, environment capture — must be inside the repo, not in a sibling Documents folder.
3. **Dependency direction stays inward-only toward `00-BBT`**, as the module READMEs already enforce. New work either feeds 00 or declares itself a side track in its README's "Relationship to the primary module" section — this convention is already followed and is worth keeping formal.
4. **Negative results and withdrawn claims stay first-class.** The Laplacian rejection, the cis/trans withdrawal, LP-002's decorrelation, SIG-003's refutation are the credibility signature of the project; the essay should have a dedicated "gates that failed" section.
5. **Attic, don't delete** (except byte-identical duplicates): historical artifacts move to `zz-attic/` with one line each in a manifest, keeping live folders live.

### Reporting workflow for "progress as a novel and reproducible work"

For each research push: (i) update ledger claims first; (ii) run the relevant `Run<Name>.wl` and commit its verification log; (iii) one dated progress note in the project's `07-progress-reports-and-history/` pointing at commits + claim IDs — never restating values; (iv) refresh the `RESEARCH.md` status column. At essay time, the essay imports numbers from the verified modules (via the paclet or `Get`), so every printed quantity has a kernel-verified source — the WSRI requirement becomes a build property instead of an editing discipline.

## 7. Bottom line

Measured against its own stated objectives, this is a substantially *delivered* research program: the correlation lens is complete and exact; the geometric lens exists with an honest decorrelation result at its center; the central black-box question has been converted from a slogan into a pre-registered protocol with a located, formalizable impossibility point; the Hawking application became a clean scope theorem on unclaimed ground; the signaling extension established feasibility; and the one open priority question is blocked at a *named* proposition rather than at vagueness. The fresh literature pass found no prior claim to the two-lens combination and supplied the exact citation set needed to defend each flank. What stands between this corpus and a strong WSRI essay is not more mathematics — it is committing the uncommitted, one formalization (O3), three scoping paragraphs (O2, D1, adversary), and the reporting layer this review adds. The repository's verification culture (pre-registration, exact certificates, adversarial audits, preserved negative results) is its most distinctive asset; organize the reporting around making that culture visible.

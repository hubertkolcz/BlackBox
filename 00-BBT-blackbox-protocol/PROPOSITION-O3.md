# The O3 impossibility statement, formalized (2026-07-13)

Objective O3 asks: *given only black-box access, under what conditions is it mathematically impossible to distinguish a genuinely quantum device from a classical optical emulation at the level of input–output behavior?* The protocol (`mbqc_blackbox_test.py`) answers this operationally via its pre-declared adversarial case (iii-d) / ledger `BBT-002`. This note states that answer as citable propositions with explicit assumptions, so the essay can assert it without over- or under-claiming. Everything below is anchored in code that already runs (`sanity-first` anchors + gates G1–G7) — no new mathematics is introduced beyond making assumptions explicit; Proposition 2 is a direct corollary of Proposition 1 worth stating separately.

## Setting (as implemented)

KCBS pentagon scenario: 5 measurements A₀..A₄ on a pentagon, contexts = edges (Aᵢ, Aᵢ₊₁), outcomes per context in {(0,0),(0,1),(1,0)} — the (1,1) event structurally absent (one photon, one click). A **table** T = {p_c(ab)} is the finite-sample-estimable object: per-context outcome distributions. **Table access** means: the tester chooses contexts, receives i.i.d. per-trial outcomes, and computes any function of the resulting empirical table (this is exactly what gates C1–C5/G1–G6 consume). The two device classes: (Q) qutrit Born sampler on the KCBS pentagram projectors; (E) *intensity emulator* — classical light divided per context, reporting normalized intensity fractions as if click probabilities, multinomially binned (per-context fractions (f₁₀, f₀₁, f₀₀) = (t+δ, t−δ, 1−2t)).

## Proposition 1 (table-level indistinguishability — the impossibility condition)

*Let T be any no-disturbance pentagon table with per-context click fractions summing to ≤ 1 (in particular the quantum-optimal table, t = 1/√5, δ = 0). Then there is an intensity emulator E(T) whose per-trial outcome distribution equals T exactly in every context. Consequently, for every sample size N and every test statistic computed from the empirical table alone, the distribution of the statistic under E(T) is identical to its distribution under any quantum device realizing T — distinguishing them from table access is mathematically impossible, not merely statistically hard.*

Grounding: the emulator's fractions are free parameters (classical intensity fractions are not constrained by single-event semantics — they range over the full no-disturbance polytope up to the exclusivity-only cap α\* = 5/2, which strictly contains the quantum point √5 < 5/2); construction (iii-d) in `mbqc_blackbox_test.py` instantiates E(T_quantum) and the protocol's own run confirms every gate C1–C5 passes with verdict QUANTUM-CERTIFIED. External anchors: classical fields *reaching* the quantum bounds is experimental fact (Frustaglia et al., PRL 116, 250404 (2016); Zhang et al., Sci. Rep. 7, 44467 (2017)); at the detector level, any click record from a single unmodified on–off detector is coherent-state-forgeable (Kovtoniuk–Bohmann–Semenov, arXiv:2601.13869 (2026)).

**Assumptions that make it true (each is an escape route when dropped):** (A1) access is to per-context tables only — no attenuation/configuration series, no photon-number resolution, no timing/coincidence data; (A2) event semantics unverified — "one trial = one physical click" is not enforced by hardware; (A3) no access to (or audit of) the device's internal compilation; (A4) fresh preparation per trial (the table is not sequential-within-run data).

## Corollary 1 (semantics escape)

Under verified single-event semantics (drop A2: heralded single photons, antibunching bound, or an attenuation-series gate in the sense of arXiv:2601.13869), the emulator class collapses to per-trial deterministic assignments — the NCHV polytope with node-sum cap α = 2 — and the quantum table (node sum √5) is certified by the existing statistical gates. The classical bound's jump under event semantics is Markiewicz et al., npj QI 5, 5 (2019).

## Corollary 2 (dynamics escape — the G7 audit and its trust assumption)

Drop A3: gate G7 (implemented; paclet interface `CascadeGenerators`/`So3Axis`/`DLADimension`) audits a *claimed mode compilation*: the KCBS cascade's stage-transition generators span 2 rotation axes and close to the full so(3), DLA dimension 3, after one commutator step (the "2 → 3 anchor"); an intensity-redistribution rig compiles to leaf-confined generators, DLA < 3, and is flagged intensity-emulable. **Trust assumption, stated plainly: G7's input is a declaration (white-box element), not a black-box observable.** The audit certifies "the claimed dynamics is/isn't classically-optically compilable," not "the device's actual dynamics is."

## Proposition 2 (no table functional certifies the DLA)

*No function of the table can lower-bound the device's DLA dimension: by Proposition 1 there exist two devices with identical tables — the quantum cascade (DLA = 3) and the tuned intensity rig (DLA < 3) — so every table functional takes the same value on both.*

This one-liner is worth stating because it fixes the architecture of the whole framework: **the geometric lens is not derivable from the correlation lens** — combining them is necessary, not decorative. It is the single-box, exact counterpart of the composition-level decorrelation finding `LP-002` (DLA growth and CF density decouple under composition), and it explains why the incumbent "contextuality-prices-classical-simulation" line (Kleinmann et al., NJP 13, 113011; Karanjai–Wallman–Bartlett, arXiv:1802.07744) cannot substitute for the DLA lens: those costs are functionals of the correlations, and the correlations are exactly what the adversary forges.

## What remains genuinely open (delegation-ready, with acceptance criteria)

**OQ1 — interventional DLA bounding.** Table access is DLA-blind (Prop. 2); *interventional* access need not be. Precise question: give the tester oracle access to tables of the transformed device, θ ↦ T(U_θ · prep), for a known parametrized family U_θ (e.g., injected wave-plate rotations between preparation and the measurement cascade). The orbit {T(U_θ)} of a DLA-3 device sweeps a manifold whose dimension is bounded by the orbit dimension of so(3); a leaf-confined rig's response manifold has strictly smaller dimension *unless the rig is allowed to re-tune its fractions adversarially as a function of θ* — so the honest formulation is a game: (a) fix the intervention family and the rig's information about θ; (b) compute max over rig strategies of the orbit-dimension it can mimic. **Probe (cheap, well-posed):** simulate the quantum cascade's table orbit under SO(3) interventions at 20–50 θ-samples; fit local rank of the Jacobian dT/dθ; do the same for the best θ-aware and θ-blind intensity rigs. Acceptance: θ-blind rig rank < quantum rank at generic θ (then interventions close BBT-002 without trusting compilations — a real result); θ-aware rig matches (then even interventions don't suffice without timing/semantics assumptions — also a real result, sharpening Prop. 1). Either outcome is publishable; the probe is a few hundred SDP-free table evaluations, no wasted compute.

**OQ2 — attenuation-series gate.** Add the arXiv:2601.13869 discrimination as an executable statistical gate (G8): tables at calibrated attenuations {η_j}; a coherent/intensity forger's click statistics scale binomially in η, a single-photon device's don't. Acceptance: pre-registered thresholds in the protocol's existing confidence-budget style; the tuned emulator (iii-d) must FAIL G8 while the quantum boxes pass at all tested V. This is the cheapest semantics-side closure of `BBT-002` that stays statistics-only.
